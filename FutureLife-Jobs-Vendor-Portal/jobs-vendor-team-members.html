Roles & permissions

Give each person the smallest role they need.

Owner
Full access: job listings, candidates, billing, team roles, and verification.
Recruiter
Create and manage job listings, candidates, interviews, and offers for assigned jobs.
Reviewer
View and comment on candidates for assigned jobs. Cannot make offers or change listings.
Finance
Billing, invoices, and subscription only. No access to candidate profiles.

Candidate data access

Owners, Recruiters, and Reviewers see candidate profiles only for job listings assigned to them. Finance sees billing records only. Read the Candidate Data & Privacy notice.
