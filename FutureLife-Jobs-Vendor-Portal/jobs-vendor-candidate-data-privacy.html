Candidate Data & Privacy

How your organisation may use the personal data candidates share through FutureLife Jobs, under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia).

Back to Team Members

Who can see candidate data

Access is limited to people with a hiring reason.

The candidate
Their own profile, applications, messages, and interview and offer records.
Assigned team members
Owners, Recruiters, and Reviewers assigned to that job listing — only for that job.
Finance role
Billing and subscription records. No candidate profiles or CVs.
Authorised FutureLife staff
Support, Trust & Safety, and compliance staff, only when needed and with access logged.

What you may and may not do

You may
Review applications, contact candidates through FutureLife Messages, and make hiring decisions for the job they applied to.
You may not
Download candidate data into other systems without consent, reuse it for other jobs or marketing, or share it outside your assigned team.
