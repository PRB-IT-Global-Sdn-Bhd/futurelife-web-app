Publish plan changes?

New prices apply to new purchases from the effective date. Existing subscriptions keep their price until renewal. Requires product and finance approval.

Live implementation

Prices, tax, discounts and entitlements are calculated in backend workflows and Stripe Prices — never from page fields. Publishing creates a new version; old versions stay in the audit log.
