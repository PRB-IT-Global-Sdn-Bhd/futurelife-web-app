When commission becomes payable

A commission record exists only after the tenancy reaches the confirmed milestone: {{COMMISSION_TRIGGER}}. Nothing is recorded or estimated while an application, shortlist, or viewing is in progress.

  1. Confirmed: the tenancy milestone is confirmed and the record is created.
  2. Payable: the invoice is issued and payment is due.
  3. Paid: FutureLife has recorded the payment.
  4. Disputed: an owner or agent has raised an issue for review.

Cancellation, withdrawal & disputes

  • Each record shows the confirmed milestone for its tenancy: {{COMMISSION_TRIGGER}}.
  • Apply cancellation or withdrawal handling according to the agreed commercial terms.
  • Use Raise a Dispute to request a review; the record is marked Disputed while FutureLife reviews it, and payment is paused only for that record.

Live implementation

Create a commission record only from a server event confirming {{COMMISSION_TRIGGER}} for a tenancy; never from an application, shortlist, viewing, or a vendor-entered value. Key each record to the tenancy reference.
