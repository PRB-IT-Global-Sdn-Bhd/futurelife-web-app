Message Support

When commission becomes payable

A commission should not be payable merely because a renter applies or is shortlisted. Record the agreed trigger clearly for every tenancy.

  1. Projected: application is in progress; no commission is payable.
  2. Pending Confirmation: rental terms or agreement are being finalised.
  3. Payable: the agreed commercial milestone has occurred.
  4. Paid: FutureLife has recorded the payment.
  5. Disputed: an owner or agent has raised an issue for review.

Cancellation, withdrawal & disputes

  • Show the agreed commission trigger for the tenancy, such as a signed agreement, first rent payment, or confirmed move-in.
  • Apply cancellation or withdrawal handling according to the agreed commercial terms.
  • Use Message Support to request a review of a commission question or dispute; do not adjust a payable record solely from the portal view.
